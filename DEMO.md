# 5-minute demo script

**Before:** MongoDB running · `npm run roads:fetch` once (internet) · both servers up · sign up with your `ADMIN_EMAILS` email (or use *Continue as guest*).

1. **Problem (30 s)** – "Maps show the fastest route, not the safest." Show the map: red/orange/yellow/green **highways, roads and zones**. Click the red highway → reasons, score, linked news.
2. **Night view (30 s)** – toggle 🌙: zones and roads escalate. "Women's safety zones weigh most after dark."
3. **Safe places (45 s)** – *Safe places* tab → filter **Women's safe space** → click one: phone, hours, directions, ✔ verified.
4. **Community (60 s)** – *Add a safe place* → pick on map → submit → shows ⏳ awaiting confirmation. Open it, press **Confirm** (use a 2nd browser/guest to reach 3) → becomes ✔ verified. Report a *Harassment* issue near a green zone and watch the colour change.
5. **Safe route (60 s)** – *Route* tab → A and B on either side of a red road → safest route recommended with the time trade-off.
6. **AI (30 s)** – *Insights* → **Generate** briefing; point at *API budget today*: "hard caps, cached summaries – the free tier can't run out."
7. **SOS (15 s)** – red SOS button → 112, helplines, nearest help, share location.

**If asked:** data is sample/demo; real data = news + community + verification (anti-spam: caps, rate limits, one vote per person).
